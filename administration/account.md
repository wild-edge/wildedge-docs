## Set your time zone and language

1. Open the profile menu in the sidebar and select **Profile**.
2. Under **Appearance & Localization**, open **Timezone** and search for a region such as `Europe/Warsaw`, or choose **UTC** when your team uses UTC to compare timestamps.
3. Open **Language** and choose the language for the WildEdge interface.

Choices save when you select them. The time zone controls how WildEdge displays timestamps in views such as Events and Runs. These preferences apply to your account. You can also set the theme and choose which companies send you a weekly summary from Profile.

Once you’re set up, [open or create a project](/administration/projects).

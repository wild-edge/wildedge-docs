# Team & permissions

Manage company membership in **Team**. An **Admin** can invite teammates, change member roles, and remove members. Roles apply at the company level, so check which company is selected before inviting someone.

[Explore Team in the demo](https://wildedge.dev/demo/wildedge-demolabs/team) · [Sign in to WildEdge](https://app.wildedge.dev/)

## Invite a teammate

1. Open the profile menu and choose **Team** under your company’s name.
2. Select **Invite Member**, then enter the teammate’s email address.
3. Choose **Admin**, **User**, or **Viewer**. Grant **Admin** when the teammate needs to manage membership or project settings.
4. Choose the invitation language and select **Send Invitation**.

## Manage invitations and members

- Use **Pending Invitations** to see who has yet to accept. Select **Resend** to send another email or **Revoke** to invalidate the invitation link.
- Once a teammate joins, use the role control on their row to change their role. Use **Remove** to remove them from the company.

The demo shows the team list. Invitation and membership changes are available in your own company.
